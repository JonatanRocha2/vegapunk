# Certification Study

Turn the current official exam blueprint into a measurable study and practice loop.

Read [references/official-sources.md](references/official-sources.md) before building a plan.

## Establish The Target

1. Identify the vendor, exact certification or exam, target date, preferred language, available hours per week, practical experience, and access to a lab or sandbox.
2. Verify the exam is active and obtain its current official exam page and guide. Record the exam code or version, guide publication or retrieval date, objective domains, published weights, format, prerequisites, and renewal policy.
3. If the exam identity is ambiguous or the official guide cannot be verified, state that limitation and resolve it before presenting a detailed plan. Do not silently rely on remembered objectives.

## Study Loop

1. Create a domain matrix from the official objectives, weighted by both exam emphasis and diagnostic weakness.
2. Run a short diagnostic with original questions and confidence ratings. Separate conceptual gaps, service or product selection gaps, and hands-on gaps.
3. Build a weekly plan backward from the target date. Include focused learning, retrieval practice, spaced review, labs, and cumulative checkpoints.
4. Anchor explanations in official documentation. Distinguish exam-relevant simplifications from production trade-offs, regional limitations, pricing, quotas, and features that may change.
5. Design sandbox labs with a goal, prerequisites, least-privilege setup, verification steps, expected evidence, cleanup, and a cost limit. Never assume free-tier eligibility.
6. Generate scenario-based practice questions from public objectives and documentation, not from live exam recollections. Explain why the best answer fits and why each distractor does not.
7. Track accuracy and confidence by domain. Reallocate time to weak weighted domains and repeat practical tasks without step-by-step prompts.
8. Run a final readiness review using timed original questions, lab completion, domain trends, and the vendor's official practice assessment when available. Do not guarantee a pass.

## Integrity And Safety

- Refuse exam dumps, braindumps, stolen questions, recalled live content, answer keys, or requests to reproduce copyrighted practice tests.
- Do not claim that this skill, its questions, or its plan is endorsed by a certification vendor.
- Use only accounts and sandboxes the learner is authorized to access; avoid production systems and clean up billable resources.
- Label uncertain or time-sensitive facts and recheck official sources close to the exam date.

## Output

Provide the verified target and source date, domain matrix, diagnostic summary, dated weekly plan, lab backlog, review cadence, measurable readiness criteria, and next checkpoint. Keep resources selective and prioritize the vendor's official guide, training, documentation, and practice material.
